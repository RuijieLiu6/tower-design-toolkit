## What was built

| Item | Vista source | Tower Design Toolkit | Changed from Vista, and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Address search | `address-search.js` | `lib/addressSearch.ts`: Vista's pure helpers ported 1:1 (query parsing, City street words, Damerau distance with a prefix allowance, `suggest`, geocoder URL and candidate checks, debounced lookup with abort, stale-answer guard, 40-query / 5-minute cache, failures not cached). The UI is "Find an address or neighbourhood" in `SitePanel.tsx` | **Privacy notice before the first query** (the one-time consent is kept in this browser). Vista's README said typed text goes to the City portal; its code sends it only to the BC Address Geocoder, and so does this port. **Offline**: nothing is sent and the panel says why. **Neighbourhoods**: the City's 22 local areas (live), because Vista's labels come from its model (studio-derived plus OSM). The search box is the Real Vancouver footprint box joined with Vista's downtown box. **"Place site here"** takes the City parcel at the point, not a bare point: the site here is a polygon |
| 2 Draw or pick the site | `site-geometry.js`, `explorer.js` step 1 | `engine/site.ts`: `validateRing` / `ringMetrics` / `pointInRing` ported 1:1 with Vista's messages and extent-scaled tolerances. `SiteRig.tsx` handles clicks on the plan (Enter, double-click or the first corner closes; Backspace undoes; Esc cancels), picking buildings, the drawn outline, the parcels and demolished outlines in red | **Picked buildings**: Vista uses a bounding rectangle, or the convex hull of the bounding-box corners. Here one building is its City outline, several are joined by the exact union when they share walls, else their hull (said). **Demolished display** plus a "Keep them" checkbox and "Replace the N buildings on the site" (at least half of a footprint inside). Hiding them in 3D waits for one line in `useRemoval` (Needed) |
| 3 Parcel matching | `import-parcels.js` | `engine/site.ts` `matchParcels`, `exactUnion` (Vista's exact shared-edge union with BigInt collinearity, no snapping), `lib/siteParcels.ts` | **Rule**: Vista counts any contact, so a wall on a lot line also takes the neighbour. Here it is exact overlap area: ≥ 95% of the parcel in the site counts, ≥ 5% is "partial" (opt in), less is "touching" 🟧. **Strata and air-space records**: 1128 W Georgia St has 4 records with one identical outline in the City layer. Exactly identical outlines are counted once (exact comparison, still no snapping). Real overlaps stay "review" and give no area. Site area for FSR comes from the joined parcels (⬜ City snapshot 2026-10-27), else from the boundary (🟧 drawn) |
| 4 Geographic location | `georeference.js`, `georeference-ui.js` | `engine/geoLocate.ts` + "Geographic location" in `SitePanel.tsx`: EPSG:4326, EPSG:26910 (and 3157), EPSG:3857; Show on model (site unchanged); Use as study site (asks first when a site exists); Use current position; GeoJSON export and import with `coordinate_provenance`, `legal_boundary_verified: false`, `vertical_coordinate_in_geometry: false` and "no vertical datum is implied" | <span style="color:#b8137a">**✖ FALSE ASSUMPTION (frame)**</span>: in Vista, UTM was the approximate input. Here EPSG:26910 is the City data's own frame (exact), and WGS84 lon/lat is the approximate one (about 1–2 m ⬜). The CRS is never guessed (Vista rule). A paste box reads "49.2857° N, 123.1236° W" or "E … N …" only when the text says which it is |
| 12 Exact geometry check | `view-envelope.js`, `cone-ceiling.js` | `engine/viewEnvelope.ts`: proposal triangles (parametric towers, stacked blocks, uploaded meshes) against each estimated cone surface (`CONE_PARAMS`, Z = Lv + k·D). The minimum clearance is taken at the vertices of the clipped part, with vertical faces covered by their edges. `EnvelopeSection.tsx` shows the controlling point and clearance per view and both numbers against the View-cone check. Exports: OBJ (the app's Y-up convention) and JSON (`tower-design-toolkit-view-envelope@1`) | Vista's surfaces are user planes; here they are the existing estimates, so there is still one cone system. A Vista bearing/pitch is never reused across frames: `fitPlane` re-fits from three XYZ points (Vista `transformPlane`). Uploaded meshes are loaded from browser storage first; if missing, the hull stands in and the panel says so |
