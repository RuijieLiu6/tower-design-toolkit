# 150-word proposal: skeleton (you write the prose)

The brief requires the proposal text to be **"150 words or fewer, written by you"** (p.4). Below are the six required points as facts to draw from, about 25 words each, not finished sentences.

| Point | Facts to use | Word budget |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | Vancouver is revising its Higher Buildings Policy; VTC asks for 244–305 m towers; entrants juggle policy, views, sun and economics across many tools | ~25 |
| **Problem** | Rules live in PDFs and Council reports; City data lacks cone heights; tools stop at different steps; results lose their sources between tools (false certainty) | ~25 |
| **Opportunities AI can bring** | AI reads policy into cited parameters and critiques its own research; code, not AI, computes heights; people decide | ~20 |
| **Proposal** | Open-source, web-first platform: real Vancouver context, categorized evaluation nodes on a canvas, community modules, presentation mode; for VTC entrants and students | ~30 |
| **Methodology** | Cited, graded sources; 3+ expert-critique rounds; deterministic engines; evaluation cases; three user sessions with VTC entrants | ~25 |
| **Possible outcome** | A working Context Model that estimates view-cone heights or says "no estimate"; first attempt already exposes the empty City fields and a 45° north error | ~25 |

**Check before submitting:** count the words; make sure nothing claims compliance ("estimates", "study tool"); credit Sinnie's and Willem's tools if they're named.
